# The First Test: How to Know What Helps

An original educational conversation revised from the user-supplied script. Short paraphrases of research, with original practical examples. No claim of full access to the listener's books. No medical self-experiment prescription. Date September 13 is a launch exception to Sunday retrieval.

## Checked references
- Wason (1960), On the failure to eliminate hypotheses in a conceptual task. https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/biases/12_Quarterly_J_Experimental_Psychology_129_(Wason).pdf . The number-rule task illustrates a difficulty distinguishing alternative hypotheses, not universal irrationality.
- Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006), Implementation intentions and goal achievement. Author-hosted paper: https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/sites/default/files/06_Gollwitzer_Sheeran_Implementation_Intentions_And_Goal.pdf . Ninety-four independent tests and pooled standardized effect around .65; not a guaranteed individual response.
- Schulz et al. (1995), Empirical evidence of bias. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7823387/ . Observational comparison of 250 trials; allocation concealment is distinct from blinding. Removed invalid conversion to a personal diary error rate.
- Simmons, Nelson & Simonsohn (2011), False-Positive Psychology. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797611417632 . Publication search text supports the song/age demonstration; direct page fetch failed. Only the qualitative demonstration is retained; it concerns flexible analysis, not literal age change.
- Kaplan & Irvin (2015), Likelihood of Null Effects of Large NHLBI Clinical Trials Has Increased over Time. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0132382 . Seventeen of thirty versus two of twenty-five. Observational trend; registration was not isolated as the only cause. No personal probability inferred.
- Guyatt et al. (1986), Determining optimal therapy: randomized trials in individual patients. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2936958/ . Supports existence of clinical individual trials, not unsupervised treatment changes.
- Ritchie, Wiseman & French (2012), Failing the Future. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0033423 . Three preregistered attempts did not reproduce the specified recall effect. Not a verdict on every spiritual claim.
- Open Science Collaboration (2015), Estimating the reproducibility of psychological science. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26315443/ . Selected 100 studies; 97% originals and 36% replications significant, smaller average replication effects. Not all psychology, and significance alone is incomplete.
- Feynman (1974), Cargo Cult Science, Caltech archive. https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/3043/1/CargoCult.pdf . Bibliographic source confirmed; script retains a brief paraphrase of intellectual honesty, removes long quotations and historical anecdotes.

## Editorial corrections
Fourteen days is a practical exercise window, not a criterion of scientific validity. Randomization does not guarantee balance in a small sample. There is no universal two-day carryover remedy. Outcome overlap is not evidence of no effect. A small result can be inconclusive. Looking away from the condition sheet is not genuine blinding. Context and dated corrections belong in an honest record. Removed personal assumptions about years of belief or actions taken yesterday. Removed unneeded Santorio, Ebbinghaus, Galton, Orne, Rosenthal, Loftus and Roberts anecdotes rather than presenting their detailed historical claims without checking each original.

The closing exercise is one observation of a normal work session; no medication, supplement, sleep restriction or financial experiment. Private diary entries need not be uploaded or shared. This episode teaches method and its limits, not a diagnosis or guaranteed transformation.
