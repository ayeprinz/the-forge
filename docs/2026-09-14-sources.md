# Sources and editorial review: Why You Repeat Habits You Want to Break

September 14, 2026. Original teaching dialogue with fictional examples. No claim to have read the full books.

- James Clear, How To Start New Habits That Actually Stick, https://jamesclear.com/three-steps-habit-change . Author's public explanation, consulted directly. Supports the attributed four-part framework and four design suggestions. Kept brief; not presented as a universal physiological sequence.
- Wood and Neal (2007), A New Look at Habits and the Habit-Goal Interface, Psychological Review. Author-hosted paper: https://dornsife.usc.edu/wendy-wood/wp-content/uploads/sites/183/2023/10/wood.neal_.2007psychrev_a_new_look_at_habits_and_the_interface_between_habits_and_goals.pdf . Consulted for context-response associations and relationships between goals and habits. Specific theory explanation kept brief; teaching scenarios are original illustrations, not research findings.
- UCL behavioural science department explanation of Lally et al. (2010): https://blogs.ucl.ac.uk/bsh/tag/66-days/ . Department account consulted; original full study not read in this run. Supports wide variation and the fact that some duration estimates were extrapolations. No universal deadline or guarantee asserted.
